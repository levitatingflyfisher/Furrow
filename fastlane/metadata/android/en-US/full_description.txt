We are what we repeatedly do.

Furrow is a daily-virtue and habit tracker for households — Benjamin Franklin's thirteen-virtues book of days, reimagined without streak-shaming or dark patterns.

• Three cadences: simple tick, a count, or a timer
• The FurrowRow inked grid — a week of days you can read at a glance
• An evening review, in Franklin's own spirit
• A focus virtue, if you want one thing to hold
• Full control: mark, unmark, clear history, or rest a habit entirely
• Encrypted backup with a 12-word recovery phrase

Missing a day is not a failure state here. Nothing is lost, nothing scolds you, and there is no streak to protect.

No ads, no accounts, no analytics. The APK asks for no network permission at all.
